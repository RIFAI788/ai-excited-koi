# Koi Leap

A giant koi leaps in a high arc over a neon-lit city skyline at dusk, reaching
for a huge glowing full moon, water splashing in its wake. A fish the size of
skyscrapers — the scale twist is the surprise, the leap is the momentum, and
the bold flat colours read clearly even at small size.
